## Focus Areas

### 1. Email Management
**Goal**: Automatically process and respond to routine emails
- Monitor inbox for new messages
- Categorize by urgency and type
- Draft responses for approval
- Handle routine inquiries autonomously

**Success Criteria**:
- 80% of routine emails handled without human intervention
- < 5 minute response time for urgent emails
- Zero missed critical communications

### 2. Task Organization
**Goal**: Maintain organized task workflow
- Automatic task creation from emails
- Priority-based task routing
- Deadline tracking and reminders
- Progress reporting

**Success Criteria**:
- All tasks properly categorized
- No tasks fall through cracks
- Clear visibility into task status

### 3. Calendar Management
**Goal**: Intelligent meeting scheduling
- Find optimal meeting times
- Handle scheduling requests
- Manage conflicts
- Send reminders

**Success Criteria**:
- Zero double-bookings
- 90% of scheduling requests handled autonomously
- Respect user preferences and constraints

## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| API rate limits | Medium | Medium | Implement caching, batch operations |
| Approval bottlenecks | High | Medium | Clear approval thresholds, smart defaults |
| Data privacy concerns | Low | High | Strict access controls, audit logging |
| System downtime | Low | High | Monitoring, graceful degradation |
| Incorrect task routing | Medium | Medium | Human review loop, learning from corrections |

## Success Definition

The Personal AI Employee will be considered successful when:

1. **Autonomy**: Handles 80%+ of routine tasks without human intervention
2. **Reliability**: 99%+ uptime with zero critical errors
3. **Efficiency**: Saves 10+ hours per week of manual work
4. **Trust**: User confidence level > 4.5/5
5. **Scalability**: Can handle 2x current workload without degradation
